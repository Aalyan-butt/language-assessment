# Kaplan administrator guide

## Sign in

Open the GitHub Pages URL and enter the administrator email and password.
Passwords are never recoverable or displayed. Use reset to assign a new one.

## Student responses

This is the default administrator section.

- Search by student name or email.
- Filter by suggested or teacher-confirmed level.
- Select **Open report** for original writing, corrected view, mistakes,
  lessons, transcripts, recording evidence and Drive/Doc links.
- Listen to all recordings before scoring pronunciation, fluency and relevance.
- Save the final level and teacher notes in **Teacher decision**.
- Use **Print report** for a paper/PDF copy.
- Use **Export CSV** for a spreadsheet export.

`Speaking evidence /5` measures only recording length, audibility, pauses and
clipping. It is not a language or pronunciation grade.

## Accounts

- **Create account** adds one student or administrator.
- **Bulk import** accepts `Name, email, password`, one student per line.
- **Edit** changes name, email, role, attempts or assigns a new password.
- **Disable** blocks login without deleting historical results.
- Increase attempts to allow a retake.

Share new passwords privately. Do not keep plaintext password lists after they
have been distributed.

## Interpreting automated results

Treat every result as a first pass. `Teacher review required` means the answer
did not provide enough task evidence for a confident automated level. A polished
off-topic paste must not be accepted as a placement result.

The teacher's saved level is the final decision.
